Ragged edge works without a boundary; with a boundary the glass fills it.
Glow shows on screen and in PNGs; stencils and cut files leave it out.
Jitter sets how jagged the bolts and branches are. Glow shows on screen and in PNGs; stencils and cut files leave it out. Fill the boundary grows thin branches into the empty space (needs a boundary).
Snap: circles that nearly touch grow or shrink until their rims meet (and meet the outer ring inside a boundary). Wobble bends every line a little, like ink by hand; it also shows in cut files.
Every node gets a large flower. Jitter sets how much the stems wave and the shoots curl. Veins and petal edges are gaps, so they show in stencils and cut files.
The glass colours show with the colour scheme "Original"; other schemes turn them into tones of the scheme. Lead is a gap in stencils and cut files, so every piece of glass cuts out on its own. Jitter curves the Tiffany lead and makes the mosaic more shattered.
Spiderweb: low jitter = orb web, high = tangled sheet web.

    About NodeLink

    NodeLink grows patterns between nodes you place: circuit boards, webs, lightning, nebulae, constellations, foam, microscope slides, arcane circles, schematics, stained glass, cathedral glass and vines. Export a transparent PNG for Procreate or print, an editable SVG, or a cut file for a Cricut, Silhouette or laser cutter.

    How it was made

    NodeLink was made by Fjallderall together with two AI models: Gemini (Google) and Claude (Anthropic). Fjallderall came up with the idea, decided what every mode should look like and how the app should work, and tested it on a PC, a phone and a Cricut. Gemini and Claude wrote the code from those directions, in many rounds of build, test and adjust.

    It started as an arcane tattoo generator and grew, round by round, into the pattern tool it is now.

    How it works

    NodeLink is one HTML page with plain CSS and JavaScript files: no frameworks, no libraries, no build step, no server. Everything runs in your browser and your designs are never uploaded. Favourites are kept in this browser only; use Save .json to keep a design elsewhere.
