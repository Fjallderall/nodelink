Ragged edge works without a boundary; with a boundary the glass fills it.
Glow shows on screen and in PNGs; stencils and cut files leave it out.
Jitter sets how jagged the bolts and branches are. Glow shows on screen and in PNGs; stencils and cut files leave it out. Fill the boundary grows thin branches into the empty space (needs a boundary).
Snap: circles that nearly touch grow or shrink until their rims meet (and meet the outer ring inside a boundary). Wobble bends every line a little, like ink by hand; it also shows in cut files.
Every node gets a large flower. Jitter sets how much the stems wave and the shoots curl. Veins and petal edges are gaps, so they show in stencils and cut files.
The glass colours show with the colour scheme "Original"; other schemes turn them into tones of the scheme. Lead is a gap in stencils and cut files, so every piece of glass cuts out on its own. Jitter curves the Tiffany lead and makes the mosaic more shattered.
Spiderweb: low jitter = orb web, high = tangled sheet web.
